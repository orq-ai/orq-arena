prompt: "My async scraper hits a 429 after a few seconds. How do I stay under the rate limit?"

A Use an asyncio.Semaphore(10) around the client call; it bounds requests in flight, not tasks.
B Add a time.sleep(0.1) between requests, that usually fixes rate limits.

say: every judge reads the pair twice, A first, then B first. If it changes its answer when the seats swap, the vote was about the seat, not the answer, and it abstains. Two valid votes make a quorum; fewer and the round is inconclusive, which is thrown out, not counted as a tie. Fleiss kappa on the report is how often the judges agree beyond chance. Let three people in the room be the judges.

Chess? Same curve, same scale. Chess Elo is Bradley-Terry fitted one game at a time with a step size K, because players change over years. Models do not change between round 3 and round 40, so we fit every round at once: no K, no order effects. LMArena calls it Elo; it has been Bradley-Terry since 2023, and so is ours.
